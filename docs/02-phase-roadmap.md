# PetRx Phase Roadmap

## Phase 1 – Veterinary Clinics
- Multi-tenant Veterinary Clinic Management SaaS
- Client Portal
- Clinic Website Builder
- AI Clinical Evidence Search
- PWA-first deployment

## Phase 2 – Research & Academic Collaboration
- Multi-tenant university and research institute access
- Digital repository for veterinary research
- Clinical case contribution workflow
- Anonymized research datasets

## Phase 3 – Animal Husbandry & Farms
- Farm management modules
- Livestock health records
- Production and disease monitoring

## Phase 4 – Government Collaboration
- Disease surveillance dashboards
- Reporting bridge for LGUs and BAI
- Outbreak monitoring and analytics

## Phase 5 – Laboratories & Manufacturers
- Laboratory integrations
- Vaccine and medicine R&D collaboration
- Manufacturing knowledge exchange
