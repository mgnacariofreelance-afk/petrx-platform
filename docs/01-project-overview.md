# PetRx Project Overview

PetRx is a multi-tenant Veterinary Clinic Management SaaS and Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) designed as the foundation of a future animal health, research, and innovation ecosystem.

## Phase 1
- Veterinary Clinic Management System
- Client Portal
- Clinic Website Builder
- AI Clinical Evidence Search
- PWA-first deployment

## Future Ecosystem
- Clinics
- Universities
- Research Institutes
- Farms
- Government Agencies
- Laboratories
- Manufacturers

Core architecture is Organization-Based, not Clinic-Based.
